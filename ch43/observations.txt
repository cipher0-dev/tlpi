1. Tiny messages have terrible bandwidth on all tests likely due to syscall
   overhead.
2. Medium message sizes had the best performance around 10k-100k.
3. Huge messages performace dropped off but was not as poor as tiny messages.
4. Message queues (sysv and posix) and datagram sockets can not have large
   message sizes. This is because they are all datagram style interfaces that
   are meant for messages that are small in size (<8192 bytes).
5. Unix datagram socketrs performed best of any test at 100k size messages.
